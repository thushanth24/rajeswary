About Our Business

Raajeshwariy Groups is a leading provider of premium wedding halls and event venues located across Jaffna, Sri Lanka. With over 15 years of experience and 500+ successful celebrations, we operate 5 premium wedding venues — Raajeshwariy Kondavil, Raajeshwariy Tellipalai, Chelva Mahal (Kokuvil), Chelva Palace (Poonary Marathadi), and Karpaka Raajeshwariy (Urumpirai).

We accept advance booking payments online via payment gateway purely for wedding hall reservations. The advance amount is variable and depends on the selected hall and date. There is no fixed price — the minimum advance payment starts from LKR 5,000 and increases based on the specific hall booked by the customer.

💳 Online Payment Gateway Integration — PayHere

We are integrating PayHere payment gateway on our website (raajeshwariygroups.com) to accept advance booking payments from customers who wish to reserve our wedding halls online. The payment amount is variable — there is no fixed product price. The minimum transaction amount for a hall booking advance starts from LKR 5,000. Customers select their desired hall and date, and the advance amount is calculated accordingly. Payments are collected in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR).

Payment Terms & Conditions
  • All prices are quoted in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR).
  • Advance wedding hall booking payment is non-refundable unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  • Minimum advance payment amount for a hall: LKR 5,000.
  • No fixed price — advance amount varies based entirely on the selected hall and date.
  • Prices are subject to change without prior notice for new bookings.
  • Confirmed bookings will honour the agreed price at the time of booking.
  • Balance payment for the hall to be settled before and on the event date.
  • Online payments processed securely via PayHere payment gateway.
  • Payment receipt and booking confirmation sent via email/SMS after successful payment.
  • Cancellation and rescheduling policies apply as per our Terms & Conditions on the website.
